# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Chase pumpkins show their flight path.** In the Story chase and in World Chase, every pumpkin Green Maniac winds up or throws draws a warm dashed path to where it will arrive (redder and wider for the giant pumpkin). The dashes flow toward you, the path is hidden behind buildings and fades out before it reaches you.
- **Chase pumpkins track a little.** In flight a pumpkin bends gently toward you and lets go shortly before arrival, so shooting it, the Light Shield and a late dodge all still work. A player who stands still or slows down is hit far more often; damage values are unchanged. Both are dev settings (Pumpkin flight path, Pumpkin tracking).
- **Arcade: never three at once.** Apart from the Fan Web row, no chart asks for more than two pumpkins inside 0.35 s, on any song or difficulty, including charts generated for imported songs and those already stored in the browser. The three-pumpkin cluster is retired (a stored one loads as ordinary pumpkins).
- **Arcade: one job per pumpkin.** Ordinary pumpkins carry the rhythm, the giant pumpkin with the face (answered by the Light Bomb) marks the song's accents only, and the Armored Pumpkin sits on runs of repeated hits and on held notes.
- **Two new Arcade songs**, *Cyber Boss: Phase 2* (150 BPM, 2:19) and *Catch the Light* (170 BPM, 2:36), with Easy, Normal and Hard charts built from an analysis of each song. The first song is re-charted by the same rules.
- The Arcade song lists show every built-in and imported song (up to six in VR); only the first song and one other stay decoded in memory.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

The first release. The game runs in the Meta Quest 3 browser at <https://bnum.magicdala.com>.

- **Story Mode**: Mobius's room, the swing through Dala City after Green Maniac with chase combat, the factory roof, and a rhythm battle inside the factory. Three in-game tutorials with video clips teach every move.
- **Arcade Mode**: a three-lane rhythm battle with Light Web, Light Bomb, Fan Web and the Armored Pumpkin with its Energy Wave, in a hall whose light show follows the beat and the player.
- **World Chase**: an endless chase across eight worlds with coins, a boss fight with a shield, close combat with the red-target punch, a finisher and a victory flow, each world with its own music.
- Hand tracking first: Light Web, Light Bomb, Fan Web, Light Shield, web pull, teleport and thumb-swipe turning. Controllers also work.
- English and Traditional Chinese text, with voiced lines for Green Maniac.
- One **Enter VR Game** button on the title page; the in-VR menu lists Story (recommended), Arcade and World Chase.
- Release build without source maps.
